## Case workflow

1. Candidate chooses service, scope and consent. Triage sees only what that workflow exposes. Confirm eligibility and expected deliverable in a shared note; request only necessary information.
2. Reviewer checks prior relationships, financial interests and competing employer/candidate duties; records a conflict/scope declaration before claiming. For a conflict, stop substantive work, escalate and arrange authorized reassignment. Do not copy the case into informal channels.
3. Keep progress and candidate corrections in shared case notes. Distinguish verified source, candidate statement, uncertainty and reviewer judgment. Do not enrich from private profiles outside the granted scope.
4. Review quality: sources/context visible, no invented evidence or hidden scoring, claims appropriately qualified, candidate corrections incorporated, next steps owned by humans. A second reviewer assesses synthetic trial cases before accepting real work.
5. Add an outcome note and obtain candidate feedback. Complete/close according to the workflow. Candidate withdrawal/expiry ends staff access; do not use exported copies to continue work. Founder handles candidate escalation through the authorized queue.
