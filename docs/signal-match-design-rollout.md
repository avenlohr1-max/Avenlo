# Signal & Match design rollout

Source: the owner-approved local package in the chat **Avenlo — Logo and Social Design**, created 23 October 2026.

The approved open A, lime signal dot, wordmark and cream/navy/teal/lime palette are integrated into the application. The homepage, candidate pathway, About and Contact pages use the approved layouts. The existing employer inquiry form, candidate plans, product catalog and authenticated product routes remain connected. Contact paths use the three addresses the owner confirmed creating.

The social-post and job-card studio is available at `/staff/studio` to active staff and founders. Its HTML, CSS and JavaScript assets require the same server authorization as its page. Drafts stay in browser local storage; exports are SVG or PNG. It does not publish posts or pull candidate, company or hiring records into templates. Sample text and role placeholders must be replaced with approved copy before publication.

No database migration, credential change, paid hosting change or DNS change is part of this release.
